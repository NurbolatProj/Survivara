# Survivara

**Survive. Upgrade. Fight.**

Survivara is a Vampire Survivors-like roguelite survival game for Android. Fight against enemies, upgrade your weapons and abilities, and try to survive as you progress through the game.

## Features

- **Map Selection** — choose a map to play.
- **Weapon Upgrades** — improve your weapons during gameplay.
- **Ability Upgrades** — enhance your abilities to support your survival.
- **Mini-Bosses** — face stronger enemies during your runs.
- **Virtual Joystick** — control your character using touchscreen controls.
- **Audio and Graphics Settings** — customize available sound and graphics options.
- **Multilingual Interface** — play in English, Russian, or Kazakh.

## Available Maps

## Installation and Running

### Play on Android

The game is available on Google Play:

**[Download Survivara from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.CrimsonArena.su&pcampaignid=web_share)**

Open the link on your Android device to access the game's Google Play page.

## Controls

Survivara uses touchscreen controls on Android.

- **Movement:** use the virtual joystick to control your character.

## Languages

The game interface supports the following languages:

- English
- Русский (Russian)
- Қазақша (Kazakh)
